🎤 Audio Device Finder

Use this tool to find the device IDs for your microphone and audio output devices for MMM-ChatGPT configuration.

📋 How to use:

  1. Click the "Refresh Devices" button below
  2. Grant permission to access your microphone when prompted
  3. Find your desired device in the list below
  4. Click "Copy ID" to copy the device ID
  5. Paste the ID into your MMM-ChatGPT config.js file
⚠️ Note: The Web Speech API used by MMM-ChatGPT has limited support for custom device selection. These device IDs are stored in your configuration for future compatibility and for alternative implementations that may support device selection better.
